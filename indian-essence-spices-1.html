Who We Are

Welcome to Indian Essence & Spices — your trusted wholesale partner for premium quality spices, masalas, chili, pepper, grains and pulses. Located in the heart of Kalamangalam, Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu, we source directly from farms to ensure the freshest, most aromatic spices reach your table.

We serve restaurants, retailers, hotels, and bulk buyers across Tamil Nadu. Our commitment is simple — pure quality, honest pricing, and reliable delivery every time.
